About

I got into engineering because I liked figuring out how things actually worked, and ten years later that's still most of the job — though these days I get just as much out of shipping something brand new as I do fixing what's broken. I've built payment flows at Simpl, Canva-like design tools at HighLevel, and healthcare and travel platforms before that: new features taken from idea to production, plus the occasional gnarly fix along the way.
